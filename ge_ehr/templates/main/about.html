Research prototype

GE-EHR for monogenic diabetes

A Genomic-Enhanced EHR with reclassification-aware re-evaluation. It stores VCF-derived variants in seven monogenic diabetes genes, classifies them against a versioned ClinVar knowledge base, applies six medication-aware rules and re-evaluates stored patients whenever ClinVar or the rules change.

1–2%of diabetes is monogenic
≈80%of cases are misdiagnosed as type 1 or type 2
50%risk for each first-degree relative (autosomal dominant)

Disclaimer

  • Synthetic data only. Every patient, VCF and demo knowledge-base release is synthetic; demo releases are ClinVar-format files that are not real ClinVar data. Do not enter real patient information.
  • Advisory — for specialist review. Recommendations are phrased for review by a diabetes specialist. GE-EHR never changes or reverses therapy itself.
  • Not validated for clinical use. The rules are simplified summaries of published guidance whose treatment evidence is limited; they must be reviewed by diabetes specialists before any clinical use.

Five modules

F1–F3 run when a VCF is uploaded or a medication or diagnosis changes; F5 runs when a new ClinVar release or rule version is imported and re-runs F2/F3 for the affected patients only.

Change-driven re-evaluation

Interpretations of variants in diabetes genes change as evidence accumulates: re-curation with the ClinGen gene-specific guidelines upgraded half of the GCK VUS in one cohort to likely pathogenic. An EHR that evaluates variants only once never passes such a change on, and a patient may stay on insulin when tablets would suffice.

When a release is imported, GE-EHR compares it with the current release only for variant keys stored for some patient, assigns each changed key a category and applies its action to every carrier — relatives sharing a variant are all re-evaluated. Work scales with the changed variants and their carriers, not with the size of ClinVar. Rule revisions re-run the revised rule for the carriers of its genes and notify only when the outcome changes.

Six rules

Each rule has the form IF an actionable variant in gene G (AND therapy condition T) THEN alert with severity S, recommendation R and source E. When the genetic criterion is met but the therapy condition is not, an explanatory note is recorded instead. {% if is_clinical %}The versions in use are on the decision rules page.{% endif %}

Limitations

  • Synthetic patients only; clinical accuracy and clinician response are not evaluated.
  • ClinVar may contain conflicting submissions; GE-EHR prefers expert-panel classifications and prompts review rather than changing therapy.
  • Seven genes and six rules: rarer monogenic and syndromic forms are not covered, and treatment in pregnancy needs specialist guidance beyond these rules.
  • The treatment rules simplify published guidance; the underlying evidence is limited and at moderate or serious risk of bias, so rules must be reviewed by diabetes specialists before any clinical use.
  • Variants are matched exactly by chromosome, position and alleles (no left-normalisation); SQLite is not suited to heavy concurrent use.
